Getting Started

When you first log in to the KMS you will land on your personal dashboard. From there you can:

  • Browse the Knowledge Repository for documents.
  • Read Lessons Learned shared by colleagues.
  • Enroll in training courses.
  • Request a mentor from the Expert Directory.

If you cannot log in, verify your email and password, then contact the KM Team if the problem persists.

How to Submit a Document

  1. Go to Submit Document from the top navigation.
  2. Fill in the title, category, document type, and abstract.
  3. Choose the criticality level (Critical, Significant, or Useful).
  4. Choose the access level (Public, Restricted, or Confidential).
  5. Attach the file and click Submit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Use the “Forgot password?” link on the login page, or email km@enec.gov.et from your ENEC email address.

Can I download documents from the repository?

Yes — open any document and use the Download button. If no file is attached, contact the KM Team to request one.

Who reviews the lessons I submit?

Lessons are reviewed by Subject Matter Experts and the KM Team before appearing with the “Approved” badge.

How do I request restricted access?

Use the “Request Higher Access” button on the document page, or contact the KM Team directly with your role and purpose.

Is the KMS available in Amharic?

Yes — use the language switcher in the header (EN / አማ). Amharic coverage is being expanded across pages.

Can I use the KMS on my phone?

Yes. The system is fully responsive and works on modern mobile browsers.
